![Sophie Potter, the society's permaculture coordinator](/images/permaculture-coordinator.jpg)

Sophie Potter is our permaculture coordinator. She knows a great deal about how soil, water, plants and wildlife get along; happily for the rest of us, she shares it. Ask her a small question and settle in.

She also runs monthly workshops on site, open to all members; each one is announced on the [news page](/news/) and our Facebook page as the date is fixed.

## What it looks like here

Compost, first. We club together for bulk compost when enough members want it, and we have been looking into collecting spent hops from a local brewery, which bags them up for exactly this purpose. A small working group is weighing up a composting toilet: cost, siting, upkeep and how to make it accessible.

Water is still mostly at the planning stage. Water butts around the site went on the wishlist, members' old IBC containers have been put forward for storage on the community plot, and someone has taken on working out how the polytunnel could use less.

Fruit bushes were agreed for the strip between Plots 29 and 30, and there is talk of more fruit trees and bird boxes from society funds. A wildlife pack, put together with the Sheffield & Rotherham Wildlife Trust, gathers rewilding ideas for anyone after some for their own plot; and the society's advice is not to cut trees between April and July, out of respect for the nesting birds.

The [plant sale](/community/) gets the same treatment: signs on companion planting (what grows well together and what should never share a bed), a starter section of organic plants, perennials grown on through the winter for the following year, and talk of cutting plant labels from old milk bottles. Sophie has promised a range of mints, plural very much intended, and a bug-hotel build for children has been on the cards too.

## Getting involved

No qualifications needed, and no need to have read a word of permaculture theory; an interest in any of the above is plenty. Come along to one of the monthly workshops, email rvascontact@gmail.com, or just start on the community plot, where most of this happens.

## What permaculture is

Permaculture is a way of designing a growing space by watching how nature runs things and joining in, rather than fighting it. It rests on three plain ethics: care for the earth, care for people, and share the surplus. None of it needs a certificate; most of it is what good allotment gardeners have always done, given a name and a bit of method.

## What it looks like on a plot

Feeding the soil rather than digging it over, with mulch doing the weeding while you put the kettle on. Compost made from whatever the plot and the kitchen offer. Plants chosen to help each other along (companion planting, to give it its name) and perennials that come back without being asked. Rain caught and kept for the dry weeks. And room left for the birds and the insects, who do half the work; pollination and pest control come free if you house the workforce.

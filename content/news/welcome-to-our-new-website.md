The society now has a proper home on the web: rvas.co.uk. If you garden at Roscoe already, welcome; if you're thinking about a plot, or just like to know what goes on in the valley, welcome too.

Have a look around. You can read a bit [about the society](/about/), wander through [our history](/history/) (people have probably been gardening in Rivelin for at least 500 years, so there is plenty of it), and find out [how to join us](/join/) if you fancy it.

This part of the site is where the news will land: plant sales, working parties, social events and anything else worth knowing. While we find our feet, the quickest way to keep up is our Facebook page, Rivelin Valley Allotment Society, or you can email us at rvascontact@gmail.com. We are always pleased to hear from you.

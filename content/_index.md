We are a friendly group of gardeners in the Rivelin Valley. Most of us have our own plots, but there is also a shared community plot and continuity beds for anyone who prefers growing at a smaller scale, wants to learn more, or just fancies some practice. Whether you are an old hand or picking up a trowel for the first time, you will find people here glad to share what they know.

RVAS is a hands-on society; we learn, share and muck in together, and good friendships come up along with the vegetables.

If that sounds like your sort of thing, find out how to [join us](/join/) or email rvascontact@gmail.com. And if you are curious how people came to be gardening in this valley in the first place, [our history](/history/) goes back a surprisingly long way.

We are a welcoming, friendly group of gardeners in the heart of the Rivelin Valley, growing plants and community in equal measure. Most of us have our own plots, but there is also a shared community plot and continuity beds for anyone who cannot manage a full plot, wants to learn more about growing, or simply fancies some practice. Whether you are an old hand or picking up a trowel for the first time, you will find people here glad to share what they know.

At heart, RVAS is a hands-on community of growers who learn, share and muck in together. It is about more than gardening: growing connections, skills and sustainability too.

If that sounds like your sort of thing, find out how to [join us](/join/) or email rvascontact@gmail.com. And if you are curious how people came to be gardening in this valley in the first place, [our history](/history/) goes back a surprisingly long way.

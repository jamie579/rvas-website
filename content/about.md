## Who we are

We are a friendly group of gardeners at Roscoe Plantation Allotments on Rivelin Valley Road, in Sheffield's Rivelin Valley. Most of us have our own plots, but we also keep a shared community plot and continuity beds; more on both below.

RVAS is where we share what we know, help one another out and enjoy each other's company. Old hands and complete beginners are equally welcome.

## What we do

The practical things first. Each autumn we arrange a bulk delivery of manure, shared among members on a first come, first served basis; woodchip and manure are free to members when available. Membership brings public liability insurance covering your plot (handy if anyone hurts themselves there; even, as our joining guide cheerfully points out, somebody who had no business being on it in the first place), along with membership of the National Allotment Society. And because the plots belong to Sheffield City Council, we petition the council on members' behalf for site improvements and the things tenants need.

Then there's the helping-each-other side. If you take on an overgrown plot, you can ask for an "hour of power", where members rally round to help you make a start on it. Working parties keep the shared parts of the site in good order; in autumn 2023 that meant laying a hedge.

![Members laying the hedge, autumn 2023](/images/hedge-laying-2023.jpg)

And then there's the fun. We meet monthly through spring and summer, hold plant sales and exchanges where spare seedlings find good homes, and get together for social events including a summer barbecue. Once a year we hold our fuddle, a shared end-of-season feast, complete with a suitably silly competition.

## The community plot

Plot 30 is our shared base. All members can use it at any time, along with the hut, the community tools and the polytunnel. We also keep continuity beds: small growing spaces for people who aren't able to take on a full plot of their own, or who want somewhere to learn and practise before they do.

## How we're run

RVAS is run by its members. A committee (including a Chairperson, a Treasurer and a Secretary) keeps things ticking over, and the society meets monthly through spring and summer.

Membership costs £5 a year per plot; see [how to join](/join/). The plots themselves are rented from Sheffield City Council, so RVAS membership is separate from, and in addition to, your council tenancy. For plot rental itself, see the [council's allotments pages](https://www.sheffield.gov.uk/parks-sport-recreation/allotments).

Use the combined printable form to complete your details and sign off once. For each tool, tell us whether you feel ready to use it, would like a demonstration or refresher, would like supported practice, or do not plan to use it. Experienced users and complete beginners are both welcome.

{{< tool-checklists restricted="no" >}}

RVAS keeps completed induction records private. **Submitting a form does not itself authorise solo use.** If you need help with a tool, arrange it before using that tool on your own.

Return your completed form to the Tool Librarian at [rvascontact@gmail.com](mailto:rvascontact@gmail.com). You can also contact us there for questions, corrections or more support.

Exact dates are agreed at meetings, and once a date is fixed it goes on the [news](/news/) page and our Facebook page. The year has a shape, though, and this is it.

**March.** The AGM, normally; the first general meeting of the season follows straight on from it. From here to October the society meets roughly monthly.

**Spring.** Sowing starts for the plant sale: members raise plants together on Plot 30 and pot on in the polytunnel. If you take on a plot in spring and it looks daunting, this is a good time to ask for an [hour of power](/join/).

**May.** The plant sale, the big weekend of our year; vegetable plants, herbs, flowers and the odd curiosity, with everything raised going back into the society's work.

**June.** The solstice gathering; we held the first in 2026 and liked it enough to agree there would be another.

**July.** A stall at Stannington Carnival, run for the community rather than the till; proceeds go to a local group.

**Summer.** The barbecue. Food on us, bring your own bottle.

**September.** The fuddle, our shared feast to close the growing season, with the produce competition alongside it; categories have included Heaviest Fruit or Veg, Longest Bean and, of course, Wonkiest Veg.

**Autumn.** The manure delivery arrives and is shared out, first come, first served. Working parties turn to the shared parts of the site, and any hedge work waits for the window between the end of August and the end of February, outside bird-nesting season.

**Winter.** The plot rests; the committee doesn't quite. Accounts are drawn up ready for the honorary auditor, and plans for the year ahead take shape.

If you'd like anything added to the year, [come to a meeting](/about/) and propose it; that is honestly all it takes.

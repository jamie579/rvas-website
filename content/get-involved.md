Anyone can help the site along, plot or no plot. For workplace volunteer days, individual volunteering, plot sharing or helping an existing member, start with [Volunteer with us](/volunteer/).

## Lend a hand

Working parties keep the shared parts of the site in good order: hedge-laying in autumn, tidying, building and mending, and the general shifting of things that allotments generate. Working days are announced on the [news page](/news/) and our Facebook page; or email [rvascontact@gmail.com](mailto:rvascontact@gmail.com) and we'll tell you about the next opportunity to join in.

If growing is more your interest than building, the monthly [permaculture workshops](/permaculture/) are open to all members, and the continuity beds are a gentle way in; see [join us](/join/).

## Schools and community groups

We've built bird boxes with children and sent leftover plants to an infant school's bee garden; we like this sort of thing. If you run a school, scout group or community organisation and the site could be useful to you, get in touch and we'll see what can be worked out.
